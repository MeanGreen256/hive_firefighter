# ADR-NNN: Title

**Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Rejected | Superseded by ADR-NNN
**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

The forces in play — technical constraints, product goals, prior art — that make this decision necessary now. What happens if we don't decide?

## Decision

The change being made, stated as a decision ("We will do X"), not a description of the problem.

## Consequences

What becomes easier and what becomes harder as a result. Name the trade-offs accepted, not just the benefits — a decision with no downside listed usually means the downside wasn't looked for.

## Alternatives considered

Options that were on the table and why they were rejected. Skip this section only if there was genuinely one option.

## Source material

Links to the concept art, spike, issue, or discussion this decision was made from, if any.

---

One file per decision: `docs/adr/NNN-short-slug.md`, numbered in the order they're written, never renumbered or reused. A decision that reverses an earlier one is a new ADR that supersedes the old one — update the old ADR's status line rather than editing its content away; the record of what was believed at the time is the point.

An ADR can be `Proposed` with the decision genuinely left open — record the options and the criteria without picking a winner. That's a legitimate end state, not a draft to be finished later on a deadline.
