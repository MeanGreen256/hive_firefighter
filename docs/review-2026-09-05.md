# Code and design review — 5 September 2026

This is a local review and implementation record, not a replacement for GitHub
issue status. The existing untracked `next-polish-and-systems-plan.md` was read
as background and left untouched.

## Assessment

The strongest next step is to improve the feel and readability of the existing
hose verb. The accepted direction is unusually clear: a child follows smoke,
drives, walks, and sprays; unlimited water and positive outcomes support
experimentation. More required controls would weaken that promise.

The August assessments are useful history but are no longer an inventory of
missing systems. The repository now contains two districts and eleven quests,
authored situation vocabulary, progression, world reactions, and production
journey automation. Rebuilding those systems from the older plans would duplicate
working code. The current game-direction document and ADRs take precedence.

The simulation/render separation, style tokens, deterministic content validation,
and regression coverage are strong foundations. Visual polish should preserve
those boundaries and the family-laptop budget.

## Findings addressed

| Finding | Player consequence | Implemented change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Target hysteresis only widened the allowed cone; angle sorting still let another flame steal the lock. | The jet could alternate between neighboring cells as aim wavered. | A valid held target retains priority until extinguished, out of range, or outside the release cone. Active manual aiming selects afresh. |
| Water beads used a fixed length regardless of distance or quality. | Long shots, especially reduced-detail shots, could read as disconnected pellets. | Length follows neighboring arc samples with overlap, retaining the existing instance budget. |
| The final arc sample computed a direction from itself to itself. | The last water piece defaulted to world -Z, regardless of spray direction. | A two-sided tangent correctly orients both arc endpoints. |
| Every water piece used the highlight color. | The jet and spray accents lacked material separation. | Style-owned blue stream and bright droplets/splashes share one instanced draw. |
| Steam was positioned at the current aim point throughout its fade. | A hot-contact puff could jump onto a cold surface or into the sky. | Steam stays at the last hot contact, rises as it fades, and clears when hose effects disable. |
| Directional sun and low uniform ambient left shaded toys and foliage dark. | The town fell short of the high-key, soft toy benchmark. | Shared style-driven sky/ground fill lifts diorama shadows without another shadow map. Ink retains its existing lighting levels. |

No scoring, water supply, progression, incident selection, or required controls
were changed. Frame animation remains outside React state.

## Remaining priorities

1. **Make art previews demonstrate real contact.** The bakery `active-spray`
   preview places the firefighter at a fixed site offset and can spray away from
   its live fire. A screenshot of that fixture cannot establish hose/fire-contact
   quality. Derive a walkable, reachable, facing pose from actual live targets and
   assert that the preview captures one before treating it as an art gate.
2. **Improve silhouettes and smoke composition at shoulder distance.** The
   inspected bakery view still has faceted foliage, simple facade masses, and
   large smoke puffs crossing the storefront. Lighting is a useful improvement,
   but it does not complete the rounded production-art benchmark. Prioritize that
   one route and its active incident before adding another district.
3. **Reduce first-load JavaScript.** The build passes its bundle budget, but Vite
   still warns about the approximately 1.38 MB minified initial JavaScript chunk.
   Evaluate loading optional adult/development surfaces separately and measure
   cold-start behavior before introducing heavier visual dependencies.
4. **Observe actual first-time children.** Automated containment cannot prove
   that fire shapes, stars, routes, or subtle target assistance are understood.
   Use the existing observation protocol to tune the changes; this review does
   not claim new child-playtest evidence.
